Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Go to the Appointments page and click "Book Appointment". Fill in the date, time, and reason for your visit, then submit the request. The hospital will review and approve your appointment.

How do I view my health records?

Navigate to the Health Records page from the sidebar or dashboard. You will see a timeline of all your health checkups, including vitals, diagnoses, and treatments.

How do medicine reminders work?

When a doctor prescribes medicine, it will appear on your Medicines page with dosage and frequency information. You will also receive notifications about your medications.

How do I update my profile?

Go to the Profile page and update your information such as address, emergency contact, blood group, allergies, and medical history. Click "Update Profile" to save changes.

What do the notification types mean?

Appointment Reminder: Updates about your scheduled appointments.
Checkup Reminder: Reminders for regular health checkups.
Medicine Reminder: Alerts about your prescribed medications.
Report: Notifications about new health reports.
Hospital Message: General announcements from the hospital.

How do I cancel an appointment?

Go to the Appointments page, find the pending appointment you want to cancel, and click the "Cancel" button. Note that only pending appointments can be cancelled.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Please contact the hospital administration to reset your password. For security reasons, password reset is not available through the portal.
